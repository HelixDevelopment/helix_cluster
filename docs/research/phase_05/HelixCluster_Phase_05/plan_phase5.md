# HelixCluster Phase 5 — Advanced & Exotic Device Ecosystem: Execution Plan

## Objective
Investigate ALL remaining categories of computers and smart devices capable of joining HelixCluster that have NOT been covered in Phases 1-3 (PC/Console/Edge/Mobile). Produce comprehensive architecture, integration plans, and documentation.

## Devices Already Covered (Phases 1-3)
- T1-T3: Desktop/Laptop/Workstation PCs (Intel, AMD, Apple Silicon, NVIDIA/AMD/Intel GPUs)
- T4-T5: PlayStation 4/4 Pro, PlayStation 5/5 Pro (jailbroken, semi-trusted)
- T6: Orange Pi 5 Max, Raspberry Pi, other SBCs
- T7: Android phones/tablets/TV boxes (Android 8-16)
- T8: iOS devices, HarmonyOS devices

## Phase 5 Research Dimensions (7 streams)

### Dim 1: Gaming & Handheld Computing Devices
- Nintendo Switch (Tegra X1, homebrew/Linux)
- Nintendo Switch 2 (NVIDIA custom SoC, 2025)
- Xbox One / Series X / Series S (AMD SoC, dev mode)
- Steam Deck / Steam Deck OLED (AMD APU, SteamOS/arch-based)
- ASUS ROG Ally, Ayaneo, GPD Win, Lenovo Legion Go (x86 handhelds)
- Retro consoles with Linux (PS2 Linux kit history)

### Dim 2: Advanced ARM SBCs & Developer Boards
- NVIDIA Jetson family (Nano, TX2 NX, Xavier NX, Orin Nano/Orin NX/AGX Orin)
- Khadas VIM4/Edge2 (Amlogic A311D2, RK3588)
- Odroid N2+/M1/M1S (Amlogic S922X, RK3566)
- NanoPi R6S/R5S (RK3588, RK3568)
- BeagleBone AI-64 (TDA4VM)
- Radxa ROCK 5B/5C (RK3588)
- Banana Pi BPI-M7 (RK3588)
- Pine64 ROCKPro64/Quartz64

### Dim 3: RISC-V & Emerging CPU Architectures
- Milk-V Mars (JH7110), Milk-V Jupiter (SpacemiT K1/M1), Milk-V Pioneer (SG2042)
- SiFive HiFive Unmatched / HiFive Premier P550
- StarFive VisionFive 2 (JH7110)
- Pine64 Star64
- Sipeed MAIX (Kendryte K210/K230)
- RISC-V ISA ecosystem status, toolchains, Linux support
- LoongArch (Loongson 3A5000/3A6000, Chinese architecture)
- MIPS (last remaining devices)

### Dim 4: FPGA & Programmable Logic Compute
- Xilinx/AMD Zynq-7000, Zynq UltraScale+ MPSoC
- Intel/Altera Cyclone V SoC, Agilex
- Lattice ECP5, CertusPro-NX
- Using FPGAs as compute accelerators (not just logic)
- FPGA cluster computing (NG-Minnow, etc.)
- Open-source FPGA tooling (Yosys, nextpnr, LiteX)

### Dim 5: Enterprise, Server & Cloud Compute Nodes
- ARM servers: Ampere Altra (80/128-core), AWS Graviton 3/4
- x86 servers: AMD EPYC (Genoa, Bergamo), Intel Xeon (Sapphire Rapids, Emerald Rapids)
- Threadripper PRO workstations
- GPU compute: NVIDIA DGX, AMD Instinct MI300X, Intel Max
- Cloud VM integration: AWS/Azure/GCP as virtual cluster nodes
- Apple Mac Studio / Mac Pro (M2 Ultra, M3 Ultra)
- Intel NUC / ASUS NUC / Mini PCs

### Dim 6: IoT, Smart Home, Wearable & Automotive Edge
- Smart TVs: Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, Vizio SmartCast
- Wearables: Apple Watch, Wear OS, potential compute donor role
- Smart speakers: Amazon Echo (AZ2 chip), Google Nest, HomePod
- Routers with OpenWrt: TP-Link, ASUS, GL.iNet (MIPS/ARM)
- NAS devices: Synology (Intel/AMD/ARM), QNAP, TrueNAS
- Automotive: NVIDIA DRIVE Orin, Qualcomm Snapdragon Ride
- Industrial: Siemens IoT2050, Advantech

### Dim 7: Exotic, Niche & Future Compute
- Quantum computing: IBM Quantum, Google Sycamore, IonQ
- ASIC repurposing: Bitmain Antminer (BM1397 BM1366), AI ASICs
- Google TPU (v4/v5), AWS Inferentia/Trainium
- Cerebras Wafer Scale Engine, Graphcore IPU (defunct but relevant)
- Mainframe integration: IBM z/Architecture, Linux on Z
- neuromorphic: Intel Loihi 2, IBM TrueNorth
- photonic computing: Lightmatter, Ayar Labs
